<objective>
Build the core of the redesign: an `Engine` base that owns exactly one adbc-poolhouse pool plus
its derived dialect, the `create_engine(config | name)` factory that constructs it, and the
registry collapsed from `(pool, dialect)` tuples to a name→Engine map. This is the SQLAlchemy
parallel — `create_engine` builds pool+dialect and the Engine holds them; `Engine.connect()`
checks out an ADBC connection; `Engine.execute(query)` runs the existing builder+`SemolinaCursor`
path through the owned pool (D1, D2, D4, D5).

Purpose: Collapse the two parallel worlds (execution pool/registry vs. codegen Engine) into one
handle. Every downstream consumer (query.execute, the introspectors, the CLI) will resolve an
Engine and run through its pool.
Output: `create_engine` in config.py (pool_from_config folded to internal, `*_connect_kwargs`
deletion deferred to Plan 03's CLI migration), `Engine` base owning pool+dialect with `connect()`
and a concrete backend-agnostic `execute()`, and `register`/`get_engine` over `_engines`.
</objective>
