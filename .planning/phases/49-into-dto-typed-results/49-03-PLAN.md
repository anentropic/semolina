<objective>
Close the one evidence gap Phase 49 inherited: measure what polars actually does with a
warehouse decimal, put the real row in the committed artifact, and correct — without
rewriting — the two places `47-DECISIONS.md` still says it is unknown.

Purpose: `47-DECISIONS.md` names this as Phase 49's problem in so many words. Assumption A3
was left open in Phase 47 for exactly one reason — polars was not installed — and Plan 01
removed that reason. `fetch_polars()` ships in this phase (Plan 05), and whether its
docstring needs a Decimal caveat is a question only a measurement can answer. Guessing would
put a claim about precision loss, or a claim that there is none, into user-facing docs on no
evidence.

Output: a real `_measure_polars(table)`, a regenerated `47-TYPE-FIDELITY.md` whose polars row
is `measured` and whose pydantic row reflects the arrowmodel-driven bump, and a dated
correction in `47-DECISIONS.md` that leaves the original text standing.
</objective>
